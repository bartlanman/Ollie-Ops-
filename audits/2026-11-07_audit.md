## What's dangerous to "clean up" (do not bulk-delete)
- Deleting a batch deletes its QBO journal entry and returns materials to inventory.
- Un-receiving a PO deletes the QBO bill and removes the inventory value.
- Deleting adjustments or packaging runs reverses their inventory/value effects.
- With QBO disconnected, any of these changes may not sync cleanly — reconnect first.
